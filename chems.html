the ss14 quick chem start guide by hoshizora aka Willow Yanagi

easy exact mixes for different chemicals you might be asked to make, no more fumbling with ratios!
GOLDEN BEAKER EDITION: jugs and golden beakers both hold 240u on Starlight, so every recipe below is sized for one golden beaker batch (max 240u). each recipe says how many batches fill a jug
amounts are multiples of 5 to match the dispenser buttons (1/5/10/15/20/25/30/50/100), odd amounts show how to split them like 35 = 30 + 5. when a sub-chem says "use N" you make a little extra to keep the numbers clean, only pour in the N
i started this site for personal use as a beginner chemist in ss14, this is by no means the quickest and most efficient way of doing things but will help you learn the ratios and get all the chems done without the CMO having to hop in and do it themselves

dispenser pull-list (optional but speeds you up)

200 iron
100 sugar
800 oxygen
600 hydrogen
600 carbon
100 silicon

jugs from the chemvend, by what you are making

airloss: 1 iron
brutes: 1 carbon and 1 sugar
burns: 1 silicon, 1 iron, 1 copper and 1 phosphorus
poisons: 1 nitrogen and 1 silicon
drag jugs and beakers onto the chem master to dump them straight in (master dump)

heat and beaker tips

if a recipe needs heat, preheat your beaker with 1u of plasma inside for a few seconds, then empty it
your beaker keeps its heat, no need to reheat it for every recipe
filling the beaker with water or fuel from a tank or sink cools it back to room temperature
expanding reactions (dexalin) cool the beaker, reducing reactions (ammonia/amoxla, benzene) heat it
alt click the chem master or dispenser to pull the beaker out fast
pull chems from the dispenser even when you do not need them yet, and tell the other chemists which category you are making
jug mixing works too, just put the reagents in different jugs 30u at a time

how it works

a cryostasis beaker stores reagents WITHOUT letting them react, it cannot break, and if you throw it the contents spill and mix on impact
load it with the unmixed halves of a reaction, walk up, throw it
a normal or bluespace beaker reacts as soon as the chems meet, so those are for staging and bulk, not for the grenade itself
modular chemical grenades are crafted in the G menu, triggers come from a protolathe or secfab, the chemical payload from a protolathe or medfab, and a payload holds TWO beakers that react when it triggers (crowbar gets the parts back if it does not go off)

what holds what (vanilla SS14 numbers, Starlight may differ so examine the item in game)

cryostasis beaker 50u, bluespace beaker 300u (reacts normally)
syringe 15u, bluespace syringe 100u (draw exact amounts from your jugs and push them into the cryostasis beaker)
unlocks: cryostasis beaker + syringe = Biochemical Stasis (Civilian Services tier 2), bluespace beaker + syringe = Bluespace Chemistry (tier 3), chemical payload = Explosive Technology

loading a thrown grenade

1. make each half of the mix in its own beaker or jug (never together)
2. syringe (bluespace syringe for big amounts) one half into the cryostasis beaker
3. add the second half last with a fresh syringe so nothing reacts early
4. keep the beaker in your hand and throw it at the target

best mixes (from the Kobold sheet, which is written for Starlight)

EXPLOSIVE (cheap, small): water + potassium. fill one beaker with water, the other with potassium
EXPLOSIVE (big): 1 chlorine + 3 fluorine + heat. one beaker HOT fluorine, the other COLD chlorine. damage caps at 150 with a 10:30 ratio
EMP: equal parts iron, uranium and aluminium, 30 of each gives a 6 tile radius. drains every battery, hurts and can stun protogens
FLASHBANG: equal parts aluminium, potassium and sulfur, 30 of each is a 6 tile radius
FOAM: 2 carbon + 2 fluorine + 1 sulfuric acid = fluorosurfactant, mixed with equal water. foam and smoke grow every 10u, so 50u is the same as 41u
FIRE: 1u of igniter + as much fuel as you can fit, see the phlogiston and napalm cards. ClF3 (1 chlorine + 3 fluorine, heated) is the other igniter but it explodes when made so make it inside a syringe

ANTAG CHEMS (from the Kobold sheet, only if you rolled antag)

rules of thumb

most species can only metabolize 2 chems at once, so never put more than 2 poisons in one mix
two poisons kill faster but give less total damage per unit injected
protogens resist poisons, use EMPs on them. a non-damaging poison (glue, mute toxin) in the mix swings a fight your way
carry mixes in a soda can or canteen, bottles and vials attract attention
vestine only comes from uplinks, everything below marked (no vestine) does not need it

poisons that need NO vestine (rate = damage per second)

heartbreaker toxin: 1 dexalin plus + 1 mindbreaker. 5 asphyxiation/s, extremely deadly injected, very deadly in foam, easy to make but also easy to heal
unstable mutagen: 1 chlorine + 1 phosphorus + 1 radium. 3 radiation/s, deadly injected, hard to heal, causes puking at 40 rads
bleach: 2 table salt + 2 space cleaner + 1 sodium hydroxide. 2 poison + 2 caustic/s, very deadly injected and hard to heal
phenol: 1 hydroxide + 1 benzene. 1 caustic + 2 poison/s, deadly injected, hard to heal, not very suspicious
amoxla: 1 ammonia + 1 iron + 1 carbon. 1.5 airloss + 1.5 poison/s, only works on non-birds, not suspicious
dexalin plus: (1 dexalin + 1 iron + 1 carbon) 2.5 airloss + 2.5 poison/s, only works on birds, extremely deadly injected, not suspicious
saline: 1 table salt + 4 water. 2 caustic + 2 heat/s, only works on birds, very deadly injected, not suspicious
benzene: 1 hydrogen + 1 carbon. 1 cellular/s, damage that cannot be healed in a fight
impedrezene: 1 mercury + 1 water + 1 oxygen. 0.65x slowdown and 2 poison/s, great injected, a good opener to stop someone running

with vestine (uplink)

lexorin: 1 heartbreaker + 1 plasma + 1 vestine. 5 asphyxiation + 5 bloodloss/s, the deadliest poison injected, easy to heal, extremely deadly in foam
nocturine: 2 impedrezene + 1 vestine. slow plus sleep, needs 8u to force sleep
tazinide: 1 licoxide + 1 vestine. 80% chance to shock each second, very good injected and in foam (licoxide = 1 lithium + 1 zinc, 50% shock/s)

contact damage (no syringe needed, stopped by hardsuits)

razorium: mix any 2 brute chems. 3u/s for 9 slash/s, 10 slash if sprayed, extremely deadly in foam, weak injected
polytrinic acid: 1 sulfuric acid + 1 plasma + 1 potassium. 11 caustic/s, 5 to 15 caustic if sprayed, extremely deadly in foam
fluorosulfuric acid: 1 fluorine + 1 hydrogen + 1 sulfuric acid + 1 potassium. 8 caustic/s, 3 to 6 caustic sprayed, deadly in foam
sulfuric acid: 2 oxygen + 1 sulfur + 1 hydrogen. 5 caustic/s, weak sprayed and in foam
range, shortest to longest: splash, spray bottle, fire extinguisher, water gun, spray nozzle. splashing does the least, spray does the most

foam bombs
